Privacy Policy

Financial Assurance, LLC ("FALA," "we," "us") respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect through our quote request form and how we use it.

What we collect

Name, phone number, email address, property or vehicle details you provide, and any documents (such as a current declarations page) you choose to upload.

How we use it

Solely to prepare and compare insurance quotes on your behalf and to contact you about your request. We do not sell your personal information.

How we share it

We may share your information with insurance carriers and underwriters as part of the quoting process. We do not share it with unrelated third parties for marketing purposes.

Document uploads

Uploaded declarations pages are stored securely and are only accessible to licensed FALA staff for the purpose of reviewing your existing coverage.

Your choices

You can ask us to delete your information at any time by contacting us directly. Submitting a quote request does not obligate you to purchase a policy.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Contact Financial Assurance, LLC directly using the phone number on our quote page.
